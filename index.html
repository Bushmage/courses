Instructional design samples

HIPAA security awareness for small healthcare practices

Four short, scenario-based courses for front desk, clinical, and billing staff. Each one opens with a realistic situation, teaches one habit at a time, and ends with a quiz and an acknowledgment.

The courses

Phishing and Social Engineering

Spot warning signs in emails, texts, calls, and visitors, then pause, check, and report.

TC-103

Safe Devices, Email, Texting, and Remote Work

About these samples

  • Built in Articulate Rise 360 and exported as web output. Each course opens in a new tab and works best in a full browser window.
  • Lessons run in order, and the quiz needs 80 percent to pass, with unlimited retries.
  • All scenarios and names are fictional. Wording such as “your Privacy Officer” stands in for details that are customized for each practice in a live version.
  • Completion is not tracked here. In a live deployment, a learning platform records completion and issues certificates.
  • These courses are training samples and are not legal advice.
